# 🩺 MediQR – Emergency Medical Identity System

> A secure, AI-powered QR-based health identity platform for patients, doctors, and hospitals to instantly access and manage critical medical records during emergencies.

---

## 📌 Overview

**MediQR** is a full-stack web application designed to help patients securely store and share emergency medical information using QR codes. Doctors and emergency personnel can scan these QR codes to access vital medical data instantly, which can save lives in critical situations.

> Built with **Node.js (Express)**, **Next.js**, **MongoDB**, and integrated with **OpenAI API** for smart health report summaries.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍⚕️ Patient Portal
- Register and log in securely
- Fill in emergency medical information
- Upload past medical reports
- Generate and download unique QR HealthCard
- Manage emergency contacts

### 🏥 Doctor/Admin Panel
- Scan or enter QR ID to access patient profile
- Add medical notes or upload new records
- View and manage patient history
- AI-based health report summarization (OpenAI)

### 🤖 AI Integration
- Summarize medical reports using GPT-4o
- Generate emergency instructions or insights
